Distance and climb

The distance shown here follows the organiser GPX checked against the 2026 timing checkpoints. Distance is measured horizontally. Elevation is sampled every 20 m and smoothed over ±60 m before the climbs are added together. The organiser’s 2026 figures appear alongside these measurements.
