Pace by leg

What it shows

Leg pace divided by whole-race pace, averaged over runners with complete checkpoint times. 1.10× means 10% slower.

Reading it

The colour key gives the thresholds; the printed factor is the exact value.

Keep in mind

Pace, not effort: stops count, and the figure resolves only between checkpoints.

Timing coverage

1,303 of 1,336 (97.5%) finishers had complete checkpoint times in 2026.

By finish rank (fastest to slowest):

  • Top 20% 265 of 267 (99.3%)
  • 20–40% 263 of 267 (98.5%)
  • 40–60% 259 of 268 (96.6%)
  • 60–80% 260 of 267 (97.4%)
  • Last 20% 256 of 267 (95.9%)

Each of the five finish-rank groups must have at least 10 runners with valid times at every checkpoint, including start and finish, and those runners must cover at least 50% of their group. Missing times are not estimated. Even when these minimums are met, the timed runners may not represent all finishers.

Where the data comes from

Trail Race Guide is independent of the race organiser and the data providers. Records from past races can be incomplete or contain errors. The official race website lists the current route, cut-offs and rules.

Course

Distance and climb

The distance shown here follows LiveTrail’s route. The climb calculation uses elevation data from Copernicus GLO-30. Distance is measured horizontally. Elevation is sampled every 20 m and smoothed over ±60 m before the climbs are added together. The organiser’s 2026 figures appear alongside these measurements. Checkpoint locations and reported distances come from the timing provider’s list.

Climb comparison

Other courses’ published climb figures come from Maarten Poirot’s UTMB Index dataset. Each course counts once, using its latest edition (2017–2024) with at least 100 participants. The distance range starts at ±2% and widens until 100 courses qualify.

Terrain picture

Terrain heights come from Copernicus GLO-30. The red line follows the course track. Use the official course map for navigation. ESA WorldCover 2021 identifies built-up areas in brown and water in blue.
