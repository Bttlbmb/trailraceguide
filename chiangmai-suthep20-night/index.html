Where the data comes from

Trail Race Guide is independent of the race organiser and the data providers. Records from past races can be incomplete or contain errors. The official race website lists the current route, cut-offs and rules.

Course

Distance and climb

The distance shown here follows LiveTrail’s route. The climb calculation uses elevation data from Copernicus GLO-30. Distance is measured horizontally. Elevation is sampled every 20 m and smoothed over ±60 m before the climbs are added together. The organiser’s 2025 figures appear alongside these measurements. Checkpoint locations and reported distances come from the timing provider’s list.

Climb comparison

Similar courses’ published climb figures come from Maarten Poirot’s UTMB Index dataset. Each course counts once, using its latest edition (2016–2024) with at least 100 participants. The distance range starts at ±2% and widens until 100 courses qualify.

Terrain picture

Terrain heights come from Copernicus GLO-30. The red line follows the course track. Use the official course map for navigation. ESA WorldCover 2021 identifies built-up areas in brown and water in blue.
