Where the data comes from

Trail Race Guide is independent of the race organiser and the data providers. Records from past races can be incomplete or contain errors. The official race website lists the current route, cut-offs and rules.

Course

Distance and climb

The distance shown here follows LiveTrail’s route. The climb calculation uses elevation data from Copernicus GLO-30. Distance is measured horizontally. Elevation is sampled every 20 m and smoothed over ±60 m before the climbs are added together. The organiser’s 2026 figures appear alongside these measurements. Checkpoint locations and reported distances come from the timing provider’s list.

Climb comparison

Other courses’ published climb figures come from Maarten Poirot’s UTMB Index dataset. Each course counts once, using its latest edition (2017–2024) with at least 100 participants. The distance range starts at ±2% and widens until 100 courses qualify.

Terrain picture

Terrain heights come from Copernicus GLO-30. The red line follows the course track. Use the official course map for navigation. ESA WorldCover 2021 identifies built-up areas in brown and water in blue.

Similar races

The distance shown here follows LiveTrail’s route. The climb calculation uses elevation data from Copernicus GLO-30. Distance is measured horizontally. Elevation is sampled every 20 m and smoothed over ±60 m before the climbs are added together. The organiser’s 2026 figures appear alongside these measurements. Checkpoint locations and reported distances come from the timing provider’s list.

Climb comparison

Other courses’ published climb figures come from Maarten Poirot’s UTMB Index dataset. Each course counts once, using its latest edition (2017–2024) with at least 100 participants. The distance range starts at ±2% and widens until 100 courses qualify.

Terrain picture

Terrain heights come from Copernicus GLO-30. The red line follows the course track. Use the official course map for navigation. ESA WorldCover 2021 identifies built-up areas in brown and water in blue.

Pace between checkpoints

The 2026 LiveTrail checkpoint times show how each runner’s pace on a section compares with their average pace for the whole race. Both figures include time spent at stops.

Similar races

Similar races fall within ±5% of this course’s measured distance and ±15% of its climb, using published figures from Maarten Poirot’s UTMB Index dataset (2017–2024). The wider climb band allows for uncertain measurement methods. Finish comparisons exclude conflicting finisher counts. The bars use matched DUV results. Every edition counts equally. Poirot supplies the overall averages. Matches check finish clocks, year, distance, field size and average time. Races with full results: 75 for all finishers, 33 for women, 75 for men.

Daylight

Finish crossings come from the 2026 LiveTrail timing archive. The line marks the scheduled start. Sunrise and sunset estimates apply to the start location. Parts of the course can fall into mountain shade earlier.

Race-day weather

The weather figures summarise hourly ERA5 estimates from Open-Meteo at the lowest, middle-elevation and highest course points during race hours.
