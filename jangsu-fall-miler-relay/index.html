Jangsu Fall Miler Relay

168.6 km · 10,760 m of climb · Jangsu Stadium · 18 September 2026 at 14:00 local time

Four runners share the Jangsu Miler course, with seven designated exchange points and a total time limit of 46 hours 30 minutes.

Teams follow the same route as the solo race. See the Miler map and elevation profile.
