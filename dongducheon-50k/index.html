Elevation profile

What it shows

Height along the route, with the biggest climbs and descents numbered.

Reading it

Green is climb, blue is descent. Point at a numbered climb to trace it on the map and in the table; select to pin.

Keep in mind

Heights from the GPX are smoothed over ±60 m, so totals can differ from published figures. Checkpoint coordinates are matched to the ordered route; an unmatched position is estimated from timing distances.

Timing coverage

Pace by leg is not shown. No verified complete-checkpoint timing summary is available for this edition.

Each of the five finish-rank groups must have at least 10 runners with valid times at every checkpoint, including start and finish, and those runners must cover at least 50% of their group. Missing times are not estimated. Even when these minimums are met, the timed runners may not represent all finishers.
